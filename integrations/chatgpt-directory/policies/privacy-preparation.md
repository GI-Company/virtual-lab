# Privacy preparation — owner review required

This is a factual worksheet for the full VirtualLab plugin's future public privacy page. It is not a published policy and does not make promises about infrastructure that does not yet exist.

Publisher contact: cory.tortorici@globalintentcompany.space.

The owner must specify the legal publisher/controller name, jurisdiction, product domain and effective date. No publisher identity verification has been observed.

## Product data flows to describe accurately

- Account identifiers, organization/project membership and authentication tokens used by the eventual service.
- Research inputs, uploaded files, experiment metadata, spectra, model conditions, results and report artifacts.
- Optional instrument identifiers/measurements and local-model interactions through a paired desktop companion.
- Job state, security logs, provenance and operational diagnostics.
- Which result fields are returned to ChatGPT and what the user explicitly permits to leave the research environment.

The existing prototype serves frozen public/synthetic MS data and excludes operator IDs, source filenames and arbitrary audit payloads from its summary tool. This does not establish the future hosted application's retention, regional storage, encryption, subprocessors or logging behavior.

## Decisions required before publication

- [ ] Hosted regions, providers and subprocessors, including any model providers.
- [ ] Legal bases/purposes and data categories for the chosen audience.
- [ ] Retention, deletion, backup, audit-log and account-closure behavior.
- [ ] Rights requests, support procedure and incident contact.
- [ ] Data-use/training policy and any consent withdrawal rules.
- [ ] Treatment of sensitive research/personal data and institutional agreements.
- [ ] Accurate descriptions of ChatGPT sharing, onward processing and third-party terms.
- [ ] Owner/legal review, public HTTPS publication and consistency checks against deployed behavior.

Do not claim “no data stored,” “local only,” “no logs,” or “never used for training” without verifying the actual product configuration and applicable service terms.
